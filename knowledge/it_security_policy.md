# BluePeak Systems IT Security Policy

## Account Security

Company account passwords must contain at least 14 characters.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for company email, source-code repositories, cloud administration systems, and the corporate VPN.

Employees must not share passwords or authentication tokens with colleagues. Shared team credentials should be stored only in an approved company secrets-management system.

## Network Access

Employees working from an untrusted or public network must use the corporate VPN before accessing internal company services.

Home networks protected by a private router and modern Wi-Fi encryption are not classified as public networks, but employees should still use the VPN when accessing systems specifically marked as VPN-only.

## Company Devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen locking.

Security updates must not be permanently disabled. Employees should restart their computers when required to complete important operating-system or security updates.

Company confidential information must not be copied to personal cloud-storage accounts.

## Security Incidents

Suspected security incidents must be reported as soon as possible.

Events involving stolen credentials, lost company devices, malware, or unauthorized access should be reported to the security team within 30 minutes of discovery.

Employees should report incidents to security@bluepeak.example and notify their manager when practical.

Employees should not attempt to hide, destroy, or modify evidence related to a security incident.
