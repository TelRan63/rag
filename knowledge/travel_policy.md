# BluePeak Systems Business Travel Policy

## Booking Travel

Employees should use the company's approved booking service whenever it is available.

Economy class should normally be used for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy may be approved for flights longer than six hours. Business-class travel requires approval from a department director.

## Hotels

The standard hotel limit is $220 per night for domestic travel and $280 per night for international travel, excluding taxes.

A manager may approve a more expensive hotel when conference location, safety, accessibility, or local availability makes the normal limit impractical.

## Meals

Employees may claim up to $65 per day for meals during approved business travel.

Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved customer or company event.

## Ground Transportation

Reasonable taxi, public transport, and ride-sharing expenses may be reimbursed when they are required for business travel.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for individual expenses above $25.

Expenses of $25 or less may be submitted without a receipt unless local accounting rules require additional documentation.

Expense reports should normally be submitted within ten working days after the employee returns from the trip.
