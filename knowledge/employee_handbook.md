# BluePeak Systems Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should submit normal vacation requests through the HR system at least five working days before the planned leave. Vacation periods longer than five consecutive working days should normally be requested at least fourteen calendar days in advance.

Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31. Any remaining carried-over days expire after that date unless HR has approved an exception in writing.

## Remote Work

Employees may normally work remotely up to three days per week when their role permits remote work.

Tuesday and Thursday are company collaboration days. Employees assigned to the main office are expected to work from the office on those days unless their manager has approved an exception.

Remote employees must remain available during the team's agreed core collaboration hours.

## Learning and Professional Development

Each full-time employee has a professional development budget of up to $1,200 per calendar year.

The budget may be used for approved technical courses, professional certifications, books, conferences, and other job-related educational materials.

Expenses above $500 require manager approval before purchase. The professional development budget cannot be exchanged for salary or other cash compensation.

## Equipment

The company provides the equipment required for an employee's primary job responsibilities.

Company laptops remain the property of BluePeak Systems and must be returned when employment ends. Employees should not install unlicensed software or disable company security controls.
